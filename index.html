How it works

  1. Intake form. A candidate applies with a PDF CV. The file is validated, saved to Google Drive and logged in a Google Sheet. Duplicate applications are rejected.
  2. AI screening. Every hour, new applications are read and each CV is scored by an AI model. Strong candidates get an interview invite, others get a polite decline.
  3. Error handler. Failures are logged and emailed as alerts. A daily digest summarises problems, and a check every two hours confirms the queue is moving.
  4. HR policy assistant. A RAG chatbot answers from the policy documents in Google Drive and names its source. A loader workflow refreshes its knowledge base at startup and every morning at 7:00.
  5. Job application coach. A form reads a CV against a job post and emails a coaching report.
